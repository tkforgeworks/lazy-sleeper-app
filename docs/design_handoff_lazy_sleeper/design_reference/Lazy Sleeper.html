Lazy Sleeper — app design, round 1
ASSUMPTIONS
12-team PPR redraft on Sleeper, slot 7. Dark is the primary theme (light via Tweaks). Compact density. Row shapes mirror GET /board and GET /draft/{id}/state; the Garage scoreboard uses your real 2024–25 benchmark MAE. ForgeModel wears the forge accent — it is the brand's own model, so it gets the spark.
IT'S ALIVE
Click any board row for the detail drawer. The draft clock runs at demo speed (×6, adjustable in Tweaks) — wait for your pick and panic mode fires itself. Garage sliders + "Re-run backtest" move the MAE with delta chips. Season Monitor (screen 4): click any week bar for its box score; the matrix shows availability + points per starter per week. Waivers, Lineup, tier sheet and Data Health are still to come.
